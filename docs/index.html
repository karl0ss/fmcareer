Your Football Manager 26 career, written up from the save

Point fmcareer at a save and a manager, and it builds one page with the whole career: every season's final table, cup runs, squad statistics and transfers, a newspaper-style review of each season, and the players and records of the era. It reads the save file itself, so nothing is typed in or copied from screenshots.

What a report holds

Career overview
A feature on the whole career, the manager's profile, league and managerial record, the climb through the league pyramid, the trophy cabinet, manager awards and the club legends of the era.
Seasons
A timeline of every season, then a review of each: the article, the final league table, cup runs, honours, full squad statistics, the season's best 18 and transfers with fees.
Players
Everyone who played under the manager, sortable, each with a page of their seasons and moves.
Records
Most appearances, goals and assists, best ratings, best single seasons, and the biggest signings and sales.
Honours & cups
Every trophy and every cup run, with who knocked you out.
Transfers
Every move in and out, season by season, filterable by type.
Club today
Finances with a bank balance chart, tactics, the live league table, and the squad by team.

A report is one HTML file with its images inside. It opens in any browser, offline, on a phone too, so you can keep it or send it to someone. Six colour themes, including a light one and one taken from your club's badge.

Download

Windows fmcareer-__VERSION__-windows.exe

Double-click it and the app opens in your browser. Leave the black window open while you use it. The first time, Windows may warn about an unrecognised app because it isn't code-signed: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

macOSApple silicon fmcareer-__VERSION__-macos-arm64.tar.gz

Double-click it to unpack. It isn't signed, so once, in Terminal in that folder, run xattr -d com.apple.quarantine fmcareer, then start it with ./fmcareer.

Linuxx64 fmcareer-__VERSION__-linux-x64.tar.gz

tar -xzf fmcareer-__VERSION__-linux-x64.tar.gz && ./fmcareer

Nothing to install. With Python 3.12 or newer you can also install it with pip and use the command line: see the README. Older versions are on the releases page.

How to use it

  1. Folders. Set your saves folder and the Football Manager 26 install folder. The app finds the usual Steam and Documents locations, and your graphics folder for logo and face packs.
  2. Save. Pick a save.
  3. Manager. Load its managers and pick one: your own, or tick "Show all managers" for anyone in the save.
  4. What to include. Choose the pages, which kinds of transfer to list at first, and whether to embed images.
  5. Options. Pick a colour theme, and optionally have an AI model write the articles.
  6. Report. Generate it, then open or download the file.

The first report from a save takes a few minutes, and a large save needs about 3 GB of memory. What was read is kept, so the same save opens quickly next time.

AI-written articles (optional)

The report writes its own articles from the data. If you'd rather, give the app any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp and others) and a model, and it has the model write them as news reports. The model is given only facts from your save. Any number in its text that the facts don't hold gets the piece asked again, then the report's own article is used. Those facts, including names from your save, go only to the endpoint you set.

Share it

Make a profile on fm-careers.co.uk and upload your report to give your career its own page and link. Upload a newer report over it as the save goes on, and the link stays the same.
