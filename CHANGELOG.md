# Changelog

What changed in each release, in plain words: these lines are the release's notes on GitHub,
and the app shows the first few when it offers the new version. Every release needs its section
(a test checks the current version has one).

## 0.0.26

- Club colours come from the save itself, so the page takes the club's real colours even
  for a club without a badge.
- The app tells you when a new version is out (Options, New versions, to turn it off).

## 0.0.25

- Players from the season in progress now show, with their appearances, goals and ratings.
- Badges are found for every club the game or your logo packs have one for, and no club
  shows another club's badge any more.
- The live league table has its real name, and a points deduction shows as minus points.
